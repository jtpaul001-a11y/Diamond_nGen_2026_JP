Elaborated Starfire nGen-400 diamond data from February and April campaigns using the Geant4 simulated diamond intrinsic efficiency. Also includes a compiled source based on these measurements. Also added a diamond openmc simulation to test that the compiled source was functioning. Graphs are shown to indicate both the flux spectra as well as the total yield across all energy bins. Although few particles were simulated, the variance is low enough to see symmetry across angles and the experimental data is correctly replicated. Enjoy!
